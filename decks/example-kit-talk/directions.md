# Design directions

## 1. Workshop Ledger

Mood: A calm, capable technical workbench that makes the process feel tangible and repeatable.
Type pairing: Space Grotesk for headlines, IBM Plex Sans for body, IBM Plex Mono for commands.
Palette idea: Paper sand, ink navy, and a single burnt-orange accent (#D55A2A).
Shape language: Strong rectangular panels, 2 px rules, a small offset index tab.
Motif: A numbered rail with a short orange marker.
Different slides: The cover is a dark blueprint-like title field; the build loop is a dark technical diagram; the sandbox slide uses a large orange statistic on a sand panel.

## 2. Editorial Signal

Mood: An assured internal memo that feels considered rather than automated.
Type pairing: Fraunces for headlines, Source Sans 3 for body, JetBrains Mono for labels.
Palette idea: Warm white, charcoal, and oxblood accent (#A33B32).
Shape language: Hairline frames, editorial quotation panels, and underscored labels.
Motif: A small red proofing mark beside every headline.
Different slides: The cover reads like a magazine cover; the quality rails become annotation cards; the bridge becomes a restrained before-and-after spread.

## 3. Signal Field

Mood: A brisk product-operations briefing with crisp system cues.
Type pairing: Manrope for headlines and body, Roboto Mono for commands.
Palette idea: Cool gray, carbon, and electric violet (#6D55E8).
Shape language: Rounded operational tiles and thin connector lines.
Motif: Paired status dots at each section change.
Different slides: The cover is a violet field; commands form a modular operations map; the final ask uses status-card check rows.

# Chosen direction: Workshop Ledger

Workshop Ledger best fits a staff-meeting explanation of a practical, portable build kit. Its warm paper ground and restrained orange marker make the technical process feel human and inspectable, while the dark system slides give the mechanism real weight.
