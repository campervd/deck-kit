Look hard. The builder already passed the mechanical checks; your job is what they miss:
- Empty areas: a band of the slide with nothing in it, a lower half left empty, a card stretched taller than its content. The right fix is larger content or a centered block, not taller boxes.
- Weak hierarchy: the headline doesn't dominate, or everything has the same weight.
- Form that doesn't fit the content: a flow, loop or system shown as two plain cards or a list instead of a drawn diagram; a comparison not side by side; steps merged that the content lists separately.
- Text on a page: a content slide without drawn components, or with only thin rules.
- Cramped or tiny text, text near box edges, uneven gaps, misaligned edges between slides.
- Inconsistent roles: the same kind of element styled differently on different slides.
- PowerPoint differences: rewrapped, clipped or moved text in the comparison.
